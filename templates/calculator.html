The ADHD Tax Calculator.

Add up what late fees, forgotten subscriptions, impulse buys and lost things cost you each month, and see it over a year and over ten. It uses the same seven categories as the worksheet in Chapter 1 of The ADHD Tax.

  1. Open your last three months of statements. Bank account and credit cards.
  2. Enter a rough monthly amount for anything that applies. Add the three months together and divide by three. Estimates are fine; this is about visibility, not precision.
  3. Look at your top three categories. That is where to start.

Everything is calculated in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
