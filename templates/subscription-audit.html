The Subscription Audit.

List what you are paying for, mark what goes, and see what cancelling is worth over a year. Forgotten subscriptions are the single most recoverable part of the ADHD tax, and one pass through a statement usually finds more than people expect.

  1. Open last month's bank and card statements. Including any second card and PayPal.
  2. Add every recurring charge, even the small ones. Small is how they survive.
  3. Mark each one keep, cancel, or unsure. Unsure is a real answer; come back to it.
  4. Cancel the cancels today, in one sitting. A list of intentions is another doom pile.

Everything stays in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
