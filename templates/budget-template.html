The ADHD Budget Template.

A budget with fewer decisions in it. List your bills and the day each one is due, give three flexible categories a figure, and get one number to check each week: what you can spend. Use it here, or download the spreadsheet for Excel or Google Sheets.

  1. Enter what actually arrives each month. After tax, not your salary on paper.
  2. List every bill with the day it is due. Mark which ones are already on autopay.
  3. Give the three flexible categories a figure. Plus a line for the ADHD tax, because it happens anyway.
  4. Check the weekly number once a week. Ten minutes. Missing a week breaks nothing.
Prefer a spreadsheet? The same template as an Excel file, with a weekly check-in sheet and an ADHD Tax log. It opens in Excel, Numbers and Google Sheets.
