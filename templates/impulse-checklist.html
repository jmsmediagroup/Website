The Impulse Spending Checklist.

Impulse buys happen in about four seconds, in a state where the part of you that weighs things up is not taking part. This does not ask you to want things less. It puts a written, dated wait between the impulse and the purchase, and keeps track of what waiting saved you.

  1. Set your rule once. A price above which anything waits, and how long it waits.
  2. Before you buy, run the quick check. It takes ten seconds and names the state you are in.
  3. Put the item on the list instead. Note where you were and what had just happened.
  4. Decide when the wait is up. Buy what you still want. A good share of the rest will look odd by then.

Everything stays in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
