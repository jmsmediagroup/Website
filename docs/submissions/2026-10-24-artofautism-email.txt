I would like to submit a blog post for the Art of Autism. It is attached, 1,327 words, in both .docx and .txt.

"I could do the job. I could not do the interview." It is a first-hand account of autistic employment, arguing that the conventional interview tests improvisation under social pressure rather than the work itself, and that a great many of us are filtered out before anyone looks at what we can actually do. It covers what goes wrong in the interview, the environment once you are hired, why masking means nobody offers you support, and where people end up, including self-employment, which I have tried to write about honestly rather than as a triumph.

I noticed October is your month for disability history and employment, which is why I am sending it now rather than later.

Following your guidelines: it is original and has not been published anywhere, it is in block style with double spacing between paragraphs, and I have sent my bio and head-shot in a separate email. There are no images in the text. Nothing in it is about curing autism or about what caused anyone's autism.

I am autistic with ADHD, originally diagnosed with Asperger's, and I write identity-first throughout, which I hope matches your house style.
