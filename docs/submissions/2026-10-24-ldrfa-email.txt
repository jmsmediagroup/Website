Hello,

I would like to submit a guest post for the LDRFA blog, following your
guidelines. It is attached as a Word document.

"The admin that costs money: assistive technology for adults with ADHD and
learning disabilities", about 1,400 words.

Your blog covers students and classrooms thoroughly, and your audience also
includes adults with learning disabilities, so I have written for that second
group. The piece is about why ordinary life admin, meaning letters, renewals,
forms and bills, is disproportionately hard when executive function is
affected, and what it quietly costs people in late fees and lapsed renewals.
Then it goes through what actually helps: text to speech for dense post, OCR on
a phone camera for paper, reminders that carry the reference number inside
them, and automating recurring payments.

It links to three of your existing articles, on text to speech, speech to text
and voice assistants, where they cover a tool in more depth than I do.

A few things your guidelines ask about:

- The post is original and has not been published anywhere, online or in print.
- There is nothing promotional in the body, and no mention of my own books.
- I write from lived experience rather than clinical authority. I am autistic
  with ADHD, originally diagnosed with Asperger's, and I raised a child with
  autism and ADHD as a single father. I say in the piece that I am not
  dyslexic, so I stay off the specifics of decoding text.
- There is nothing in it about medication or treatment.
- Please edit it as you see fit.

I can send it as a Google Doc or as HTML instead if either is easier.
