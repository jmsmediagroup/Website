About the author

J.M. Struijk

J.M. Struijk writes practical books for adults with ADHD, autism, or both. J.M. was diagnosed as an adult with Asperger's (now part of the autism spectrum) and ADHD, and has raised a son alone since the boy was about three. The books come from that experience and from published research. J.M. is not a clinician.

Common questions

Where to start, and what these books are

Which J.M. Struijk book should I start with?

Start with the book for the problem that costs you most right now. For money (late fees, forgotten subscriptions, impulse spending) that is The ADHD Tax. For thoughts that loop, it is The ADHD Overthinking Reset. If you were diagnosed as an adult, start with The Late-Diagnosed ADHD Workbook or The Late-Diagnosed Autistic Workbook. For exhaustion that rest doesn't fix, start with The ADHD Burnout Recovery Guide. The start here page sorts this by situation.

Do I have to read the series in order?

No. The books in each series are numbered, but each one stands on its own and assumes you haven't read the others. The library page shows which book is a good first one in each group and which books go with it.

Are these books for ADHD, autism, or both?

It depends on the title. The money books and the ADHD Reset Series are written for adults with ADHD. The Late-Diagnosed Autistic Workbook and The Autism Masking Reset are for autistic adults, and The Late-Diagnosed AuDHD Workbook is for people who are both.

What does AuDHD mean?

AuDHD is an informal word for being both autistic and ADHD. The two often occur together, and the combination can bring needs that pull against each other, such as wanting routine and craving novelty, which advice written for only one of them tends to miss.

Is the toolkit really free?

Yes. It is a free printable PDF sent by email. Signing up adds you to the J.M. Struijk email list, and every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. The books are complete without it.

Where can I buy the books, and in which formats?

The books are sold on Amazon. Each book page on this site lists the formats that exist (paperback, Kindle, hardcover or audiobook) with a button for each. This site doesn't sell books itself, and nothing recommended on it pays the author or the publisher.

Are these books a substitute for diagnosis or treatment?

No. They are practical self-help books written by a non-clinician from lived experience and published sources. They don't diagnose anything and don't replace a doctor, therapist or other qualified professional.
