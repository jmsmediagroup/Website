Everyone else at the table has ordered. You have been looking at the menu for ten minutes and still cannot decide. It is rarely about the food.

ADHD changes how decisions get processed, which is why ordinary choices can feel overwhelming and why "just pick one" does not help. This book gives you fixed protocols that turn everyday decisions into something you can finish in two to ten minutes. It is the third book in the ADHD Reset Series.

## Who it's for

Anyone with ADHD who has spent twenty minutes choosing a meal, weeks researching a purchase without buying, or years "figuring out" a career. Also anyone who freezes in front of a to-do list because every item looks equally urgent.

## What's inside

The core of the book is three protocols matched to the size of the decision:

- The 2-Minute Decision Protocol for everyday choices like what to eat, wear or do.
- The 5-Minute Decision Protocol for medium decisions such as purchases and plans.
- The 10-Minute Decision Protocol, a structured first pass at bigger choices about work and relationships.

Around those sit the Emergency Decision Interrupt for when you are frozen, a method for removing most routine daily decisions altogether, and a "good enough" standard for perfectionism paralysis. It works through specific traps: food, shopping, prioritising tasks, career and life decisions, and avoiding difficult conversations.

The goal is not to turn you into a decisive person. It is to make decisions fast enough to get on with your life.

The fast protocols suit choices you can undo. A decision that is expensive, hard to reverse, or made during a crisis or a low patch deserves more time than this book gives it: set a date a few days out, check what it would cost to undo, and talk it through with someone you trust.

## Common questions

### Does it help with big decisions or only small ones?

The book is strongest on small and medium choices. It has a 10-minute protocol for bigger decisions about career and relationships, but treat that as a first pass. For anything costly or hard to undo, take days over it. The relationship chapter does not cover safety planning. If a relationship is abusive, leaving safely takes a plan, so talk to an advocate first: the National Domestic Violence Hotline is at 1-800-799-7233, by text (START to 88788) or by chat at thehotline.org. If you are in danger now, call 911.

### What is the "good enough" standard?

It is the book's approach to perfectionism paralysis: choosing a level of quality that is sufficient instead of searching for the best possible option.

### Is decision paralysis an ADHD thing?

Difficulty making decisions is commonly reported by adults with ADHD and is linked to executive function. It also overlaps with anxiety and depression, so if indecision is part of broader, persistent worry or low mood, it is worth discussing with a professional.
