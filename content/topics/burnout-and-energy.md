Neurodivergent brains tend to cost more to run. Masking, filtering sensory input, starting tasks through sheer effort, compensating for difficulties nobody else can see: all of it draws on the same limited supply, and most of it does not register as work. That is why burnout so often seems to come from nowhere.

The articles here are about that supply. How to tell autistic burnout from ADHD burnout and from depression, which need very different responses. Why a brilliant hyperfocus day can cost you the rest of the week. Why sleep keeps sliding later, and why a meltdown or a shutdown says something about load rather than character.

The thread running through all of them is that rest only works when it matches the kind of depletion, and that energy is a more useful thing to manage than time.
