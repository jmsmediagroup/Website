Finding out as an adult that you are ADHD, autistic, or both tends to arrive as two things at once. There is relief, because a lifetime of feeling slightly out of step suddenly has a reason. And there is grief, for the years spent believing the problem was your character, and for the version of you who tried so hard at things that were never going to work the way they were being attempted.

This is the collection for that stage, whether you are still wondering, waiting for an assessment, or a few months past the answer. It covers how adult assessment works and how to prepare for it, what online tests can and cannot tell you, why so many women in particular were missed, and what the first year after finding out is usually like.

If you are only starting to wonder, read [what online tests can tell you](/blog/online-adhd-autism-tests/) and [how to get assessed as an adult](/blog/adult-adhd-autism-assessment/). If you already know, [the first year after an ADHD diagnosis](/blog/adhd-diagnosis-as-an-adult-first-year/) and [finding out you are autistic](/blog/autistic-in-adulthood-late-diagnosis/) are where most people find themselves.
