The ADHD Budget Planner

The Undated 12-Month Money Management System for Adults with ADHD

Published 8 August 2026·By J.M. Struijk
View on Amazon

Kindle and print editions, where available, are listed on the Amazon page.

Dated planners have a built-in failure point: the first skipped week. For an ADHD brain that week always arrives, and then the planner goes in a drawer with the guilt attached.

This one is undated. You name the months yourself, you can start in any of them, and missing one does not break anything.

Who it's for

Adults with ADHD who have abandoned planners before and want a money system that waits for them when they come back. It also suits anyone who has built a budget system and needs somewhere to keep it running year after year.

What's inside

It opens with a one-hour Setup section, which does most of the heavy lifting:

  • A Bills Master List that gets every due date out of your head and onto paper.
  • The Subscription Hunt, for finding charges you forgot about and reclaiming that money.
  • The Autopilot Checklist, covering autopay, moved due dates and payment confirmations.

Then there are twelve identical undated months. Each has a one-page Money Map of the month's budget, a bill due-date tracker, four 10-minute weekly check-ins, an ADHD Tax log that records late fees, impulse buys and forgotten subscriptions, and a month review. Quarterly pit stops use a one-change rule to stop you rebuilding the whole system instead of using it.

For the hard moments there is a Missed-Payment page that scripts the call asking for a fee to be waived, a Restart page for when you find the planner after weeks away, and colourable progress trackers.

Where it fits

The planner is the long-term home for a system. The ADHD Budget Workbook is the guided 90-day reset that builds it. The ADHD Debt Payoff Workbook handles debt specifically, and The ADHD Tax explains the patterns all of it is designed around.

Common questions

Why is the planner undated?

Because the skipped week is where dated planners fail. Undated months mean you can start any time, miss a month, and continue without blank pages reminding you of the gap.

How long does setup take?

The Setup section is designed to take about an hour. After that, each week needs a 10-minute check-in.

Do I need the Budget Workbook first?

No, the planner works on its own. The workbook is useful if you want a guided program to build the system before you start maintaining it in the planner.
