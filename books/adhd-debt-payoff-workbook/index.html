The ADHD Debt Payoff Workbook

A Step-by-Step Workbook to Help You Pay Off Debt, Build Momentum, and Create Financial Freedom

Published 8 August 2026·By J.M. Struijk
View on Amazon

Kindle and print editions, where available, are listed on the Amazon page.

Every debt plan most of us have quit relied on exactly what ADHD makes hardest: remembering due dates, resisting the pull of spending, feeling consequences that are months away, and doing a boring task the same way for years.

This workbook does not ask your brain to change. It replaces memory with autopilot, puts visible wins on a weekly schedule, and gives you restart pages for the times you disappear.

Who it's for

Anyone with ADHD carrying debt they have tried and failed to clear, whether that is credit cards, buy-now-pay-later accounts, medical bills, or all of them at once. It is written for the reality of a pile of unopened post and plans that always start on Monday.

What's inside

It is a six-month workbook. You will:

  • Face every debt in one sitting with the "Envelope Amnesty", where estimates are allowed and shame is not required.
  • Choose a payoff method in two minutes with a short quiz: snowball, avalanche, or the ADHD Hybrid.
  • Put every payment on autopilot so late fees stop from the first day.
  • Run 10-minute weekly check-ins designed to survive bad weeks.
  • Colour in debt thermometers that make progress hard to ignore.
  • Use Missed-Payment and Restart pages when life gets in the way.

There are no spreadsheets to maintain and no 47-category budgets. The system assumes you will vanish for three weeks at some point and keeps working when you come back.

Where it fits

The ADHD Tax introduces the patterns behind ADHD money problems, including debt. This workbook is the dedicated tool for paying debt down. Once you are through it, The ADHD Budget Planner keeps the rest of your money running. The article on why payoff plans keep failing explains the thinking behind the approach.

Common questions

Snowball or avalanche: which does the workbook use?

You choose. A short quiz at the start helps you pick between the snowball method, the avalanche method, or the ADHD Hybrid, depending on what will keep you going.

What if I miss a payment or abandon the workbook for a while?

The workbook expects that. There is a Missed-Payment page and a Restart page specifically for coming back after a gap, without starting from zero.

How long does it take?

The workbook is structured around six months, with a 10-minute check-in each week.
