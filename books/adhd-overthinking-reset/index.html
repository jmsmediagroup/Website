The ADHD Overthinking Reset

Break Free from Mental Loops, Quiet Intrusive Thoughts, and Stop Spiraling — Even When Your Brain Won't Shut Up

Published 31 December 2025·69 pages·By J.M. Struijk

It is two in the morning and your brain is replaying a conversation from three days ago. You are tired, you know none of this matters at this hour, and the loop keeps running anyway.

The usual advice, to breathe or think positively or journal it out, tends to fall apart the moment you are actually inside the spiral. This book is a set of fast interventions for that moment, written for ADHD brains. It is the first book in the ADHD Reset Series.

Who it's for

Anyone with ADHD who gets caught in mental loops: replaying conversations, spiralling over what someone meant, lying awake with racing thoughts. It is especially aimed at the spirals that hit ADHD brains hardest, including rejection sensitivity, money anxiety, productivity guilt, relationship overthinking and nighttime overthinking.

What's inside

The chapters are short and the tools are meant to be used in the moment:

  • The 90-Second Interrupt, for breaking a loop before it takes over your day.
  • The 5-Minute Grounding Protocol, for when overstimulation is pushing you toward shutdown.
  • How to handle intrusive thoughts without fighting them or feeding them.
  • How to make decisions without needing perfect certainty first.
  • Specific approaches for rejection sensitive dysphoria, money anxiety, productivity guilt, relationship overthinking and sleepless nights.
  • A prevention system that does not require a perfect morning routine.

The goal is not to stop spiralling forever. It is to catch rumination earlier, recover faster, and treat yourself more kindly along the way.

Where it fits

The Reset Series takes one stuck pattern per book. The ADHD Eating Reset covers meal paralysis and The ADHD Decision Reset covers analysis paralysis. For more on the nighttime version of this problem, read the 2 a.m. spiral, and for the emotional side, rejection sensitive dysphoria.

Common questions

How long is the book?

It is short on purpose, around 69 pages in print length on Amazon, with brief chapters you can use when your attention is limited.

Does it cover rejection sensitive dysphoria?

Yes. RSD is one of the specific spirals the book addresses, alongside money anxiety, productivity guilt, relationship overthinking and nighttime overthinking.

Is it about meditation?

No. The tools are quick, practical interrupts rather than meditation practices or long routines.
