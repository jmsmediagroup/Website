Free tools

Tools that do the boring part.

Free, no sign-up, nothing stored on a server. Each one removes a step that ADHD makes expensive: adding things up, holding a list in your head, or deciding the same thing twice.

Interactive and .xlsx

ADHD Budget Template

Bills with due days and autopay, three flexible categories and one weekly number. Use it online or download it for Excel and Google Sheets.

 Interactive

ADHD Tax Calculator

Add up what late fees, forgotten subscriptions, impulse buys and lost things cost you, by category, and see it over a year and over ten.

 Interactive

Subscription Audit

List every recurring charge, mark what goes, and see what cancelling is worth over a year.

 PDF, A4 and US Letter

The ADHD Tax printable worksheets

Five printable worksheets from the book: the ADHD Tax Calculator, a money personality quiz, a trigger map, a 48-hour action plan and a monthly check-in.
